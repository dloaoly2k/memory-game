How to Play:

  • Enter your name and click "Save" to personalize your game
  • Select a difficulty level (1-5) - higher levels have more cards
  • Adjust sound settings using the volume controls
  • Click "Start Game" to begin
  • Flip two cards at a time to find matching pairs
  • Matched pairs stay face up
  • Complete all pairs to advance to the next level!
